2026 BALLON D`OR WINNERS

1:HARRY KANE

Harry Kane won the 2026 Ballon d'Or after an outstanding season for Bayern Munich and England. He scored 73 goals for club and country, helped Bayern win a domestic treble, finished as the Bundesliga's top scorer, and scored 14 goals in the UEFA Champions League. These achievements earned him football's most prestigious individual award.

2:KYLIAN MBAPPE

Kylian Mbappé finished in 2nd place in the 2026 Ballon d'Or rankings, behind Harry Kane. Throughout the season, Mbappé was one of the world's standout performers for Real Madrid and France, producing an exceptional level of consistency and goalscoring. Although he narrowly missed out on the award, his performances were strong enough to earn him second place in the final voting.

3:LAMINE YAMAL

Lamine Yamal finished third in the 2026 Ballon d'Or rankings and was also named Men's Young Talent of the Year. Despite being one of the youngest players among the nominees, the Barcelona star continued to impress with his performances for both club and country. His talent, consistency, and impact at such a young age helped him secure a place on the Ballon d'Or podium and establish himself as one of football's brightest young stars.
